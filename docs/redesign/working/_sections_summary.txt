===== 10-balance

The canon's constants run away. The first nuke comes at 22 minutes (N1 wants 40-60). The active player nukes 16 times on day 1, and the optimal player lights the whole tree by day 12. The cause: while rungs, eras and upgrades are still ahead, supplies per run grow as power^3.9, and the cube-root formula turns that into a cascade.

- Rush ×5;
- crew ranks ×2 that rise together;
- a crown that also fires when the nuke counts and the run is 20 hours old;
- Late Tide ×3;
- a per-ring Blast Map budget.
