What this tells us

The pro model is the one. Side view, transparent, one grid, no fringe, first try. 0.18 USD a sprite means the whole scene, four eras of targets, hands and busts land well inside the budget.
Bands need a different route. The environment style paints postcards. For the sky, sea and ground the game will use the palette gradient plus small generated pieces (clouds, a water tile, a ground tile), or the tileset styles, which exist for exactly this.
Palette drift is visible. The bench is grey-brown, the shelter cooler, the car warmer. The 48-colour palette and the palette-constrain option fix that in the real pass; this mock skipped it on purpose.
Animation works. The idle and walking styles took the finished survivor as input and returned coherent 8-frame cycles for 0.14 USD each. Hands, flotsam bobbing and lamp sway all go this way.
Not a reference yet. Nothing here goes into the repo. R2 Feel's art sprint regenerates with the palette and a consistent light direction, and the owner picks by number from contact sheets like the one above.
